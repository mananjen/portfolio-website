# Portfolio

The portfolio explains Manan's work and contributions to engineers and hiring
managers, alongside a personal view of his interests.

## Language

**Project**:
An independently pursued, academic, or research body of work presented on its
own merits. Work performed as an industry employee is described within its role
unless deliberately selected as a separate project.

**Role**:
An employment or research position with organizational context, responsibilities,
and contributions. A research role and a research project can describe the same
work from different perspectives without repeating the entire account.

**Project preview**:
A compact introduction that helps a reader decide whether to explore a project.

**Project brief**:
The fuller account of a project's problem, contribution, meaningful decisions,
and supported outcomes. Its length follows the available substance.

**Featured project**:
A project selected for greater editorial prominence. Featured status expresses
emphasis, not recency or an entitlement to more substantive detail.
